## Final generation prompts

### phone

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: one square website illustration for a matching three-image set. Art direction: editorial mixed-media product collage, an actual photorealistic electronic device cut out like a catalog photograph, with one large loose imperfect hand-drawn charcoal looping scribble weaving behind and just a little in front of it. Flat warm ivory background #fcf9ee. Palette strictly warm cream, sage #93b17e, muted mint, charcoal #252623 and ivory whites. Tiny sage patches are fine. Matte realistic materials and crisp real photography details, no cute toy 3D look. Center the complete device with generous 10% margins and visible open looping lines around it, similar to a music equipment ad with a photographic keyboard over a loose black doodle. No pink, no neon, no brand logos, no large text, no people, no room scene, no watermark. Keep background flat, subtle photographic contact shadow only. Make it punchy yet calm and compatible with an elegant cream-and-sage music practice website. Subject: one modern black smartphone in portrait orientation, very slightly tilted, seen mostly from the front. Warm ivory screen shows convincing black sheet music on staves, a slim sage current-note highlight, and small piano keys along the bottom. Phone itself is tall and fills 75% of canvas height. One lively charcoal pencil loop runs around phone edges. No label or caption baked into the art.
